_We introduce a family of fibre-cofibre sequences in the category of exact $$\infty$$-categories generalising the notion of Verdier sequences of stable $$\infty$$-categories, and study their $$K$$-theoretic behaviour: we show that connective algebraic $$K$$-theory is Keller localising, and use this to give a categorical proof of the Gillet-Waldhausen theorem. We also give a self-contained proof of Waldhausen's additivity theorem for exact $$\infty$$-categories._
