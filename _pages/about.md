Hi! I am a mathematician and teaching fellow at the [Warwick Mathematics Institute](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/), where I recently completed a PhD under the supervision of [Dr. Marco Schlichting](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/people/staff/marco_schlichting/) (my Ph.D. profile can be found [here](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/people/staff/marlowe/)).

My research interests lie in homotopy theory, and more specifically in invariants such as higher algebraic and hermitian $$K$$-theory, and the application of these to motivic homotopy theory.

Before Warwick, I was briefly a programmer for a contract research organisation specialising in the analysis of clinical trials data; before this, I was an MSc student at the University of Nottingham, and wrote a thesis on the norm residue theorem (in degree 2) relating Milnor $$K$$-theory and Galois cohomology under the supervision of [Professor Alexander Vishik](https://www.maths.nottingham.ac.uk/plp/pmzav/). Before this, I was an undergraduate student at Trinity Hall, University of Cambridge.
